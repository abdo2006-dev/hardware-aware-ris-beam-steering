## Frequency and pitch

The paper describes operation around 28 GHz and reports a balanced operating point near 26.168 GHz. At 26.168 GHz, a 5.35 mm pitch is approximately 0.467 lambda0; at 28 GHz it is approximately a half free-space wavelength. This project retains the reported 5.35 mm pitch and uses 26.168 GHz for the array-factor comparison without forcing the two values into an exact half-wavelength relation.
